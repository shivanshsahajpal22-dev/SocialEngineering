# 🎭 Social Engineering

Notes and write-ups on the **human side of red teaming** — pretexting, phishing campaign design, OSINT-driven targeting, and vishing. This repo documents methodology and lessons from authorized engagements, not scripts for unauthorized use.

> ⚠️ **For educational and authorized-engagement use only.** Social engineering without explicit, informed consent is illegal in most jurisdictions and causes real harm to real people. Everything here assumes a signed rules-of-engagement (RoE) with defined scope, targets, and limits.

## 🧠 Philosophy

Social engineering write-ups here follow a consistent structure:

1. **Objective** — what the engagement was trying to test (not just "get access")
2. **Pretext design** — the reasoning behind the chosen approach and why it fit the target
3. **Execution notes** — what worked, what didn't, and why
4. **Detection & awareness takeaway** — how the same technique could be spotted or reported

The point isn't the trick itself — it's understanding *why people trust certain signals*, so both attackers and defenders can reason about it properly.
