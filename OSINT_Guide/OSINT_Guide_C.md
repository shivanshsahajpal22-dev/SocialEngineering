# Template for OSINT games 

`These are Games you can play with your friend who is interested in OSINT too.`

Rules 
1. Set a timer or duration for each round
2. Different Information found has different points depending on the sensitivity and how hard the information IS! 
3. Whoever scores the most points wins the OSINT round

## Template for HUMINT
`We have a person blended in with the normal people; you have to convince him to give you the flag. He would almost act like a normal person., you might be able to pick a random person until you don't turn this into harassment`
```
SETUP
One player is the TARGET — briefed with a secret flag phrase/word
and told to act like an ordinary, sociable person. The Target should
NOT stonewall everything — they should be genuinely gettable through
good conversation, just not someone who'd casually blurt it out
unprompted. Everyone else is an OPERATOR trying to extract the flag
through normal-sounding conversation, without the Target realizing
that's what's happening.

Round length                       : [// fill here, suggest 10-15 min]

SCORING — OPERATOR SIDE

TIER 1
Target reveals a partial/adjacent
hint without realizing it           : [// fill here] -- 3 points
Target confirms a guessed
fragment of the flag                : [// fill here] -- 4 points

TIER 2
Full flag extracted                 : [// fill here] -- 10 points
Extracted using an identifiable
textbook elicitation technique
(bracketing, assumed knowledge,
deliberate wrong statement, etc —
judge names which one was used)     : [// fill here] -- +3 bonus points

TIER 3 (style/tradecraft bonus)
Target never suspected anything
was being extracted from them       : [// fill here] -- +5 points
Extraction done entirely through
questions, no direct ask at any
point in the conversation           : [// fill here] -- +4 points

PENALTIES (Operator)
Target became visibly suspicious
or the conversation broke down      : -3 points
Direct/obvious ask ("what's the
flag") used at any point            : -5 points

SCORING — TARGET SIDE (makes this two-sided, not just one team
"winning" by default)

Full round survived, flag never
given up                            : [// fill here] -- 8 points
Correctly identified and named
the specific technique an Operator
used against them, after the round  : [// fill here] -- +4 points
Gave a false/misleading flag
fragment that an Operator wrote
down as if confirmed                : [// fill here] -- +5 points

DEBRIEF (do this every round, it's the actual learning part)
- Operator explains which technique they used and why they picked it
- Target explains what almost worked / what they noticed
- Judge (if present) confirms which MICE/RASCLS lever was actually
  in play, since players often don't consciously clock it themselves 
```
